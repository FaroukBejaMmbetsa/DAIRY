A little about us

Built around the work
behind good food.

Dairy Management is a student-built farm operations concept designed to bring the everyday work of a dairy closer together: livestock, production, sales, and the people who make it happen.

We care about clear records, dependable service, and making local farm products easier to find and order. This storefront is the customer-facing part of that idea.

  • Care firstHealthy animals and responsible handling.
  • Keep it clearSimple records that help people make good decisions.
  • Grow togetherA practical system shaped by the whole group.
